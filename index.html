NutraTrim

It's not what you eat, it's when you eat it.

NutraTrim is a meal clock for iPhone. Tell it what you burn, pick a deficit, and it plans your day: when to eat, how many calories each meal gets, and fewer carbs as the day goes on. At each meal it sends a reminder: "Time to eat! Eat 430 calories in the next half hour", with food ideas that fit. When the kitchen closes, it says so.

  • Calories in, calories out: a daily deficit you choose, never below 1,200 calories
  • Bigger meals early, lower-carb meals late, nothing after 7 PM or close to bedtime
  • Meal reminders with food ideas sized to the calories
  • Scan your plate: photograph your food and see how much of it fits the meal
  • Workouts widen your deficit or let you eat a little more, your choice, with Apple Health and Apple Watch
  • A 24-hour clock, a daily balance and a streak for days on plan

NutraTrim is a planning tool for healthy adults, not medical advice. Talk to your doctor before cutting calories if you are pregnant or nursing, have diabetes or another condition, or have a history of eating disorders.
