Questions

Good to know

Is NutraTrim a diet?

No. You can eat what you like. NutraTrim decides how much and when: a daily calorie deficit, bigger meals early, fewer carbs late, and nothing after the kitchen closes. Every meal comes with ideas if you want them.

How does it know how many calories I burn?

It starts with a simple rule of thumb: your weight in pounds times ten, a rough resting estimate. If you know your real number from a tracker or your doctor, type it in. Workouts can add to it, logged by hand or read from Apple Health.

Why no eating after 7 PM?

That's the idea behind the app: front-load your calories and give your body a long overnight break. The kitchen closes at 7 PM or three hours before bed, whichever comes first, and you can change the time.

How accurate is the plate scanner?

It's a good estimate, not a scale. Portion size is the hard part, so results can be off by 20% or more. Shoot from above in good light with the whole plate in frame, and add a hint like "fried in butter" for what the camera can't see.

What happens to my data?

Your plan, your log and your Apple Health data stay on your iPhone. There's no account, no ads and no tracking. The only thing that leaves your phone is a plate photo you choose to scan, which is used for the estimate and not kept. Read the privacy policy.

Is it safe for everyone?

NutraTrim is for healthy adults. It never sets a target below 1,200 calories a day. If you are pregnant or nursing, under 18, have diabetes or another medical condition, or have a history of eating disorders, talk to your doctor first.

When is it on Android?

NutraTrim is coming to iPhone first, with Android to follow. Both store links will appear on this page as soon as they're live.
